Where frontier AI still breaks

At Halluminate, a Y Combinator startup that builds reinforcement learning environments for financial services, I wrote problems that frontier models could not solve. At peak, I was the top author among about 300 expert consultants. Two failure patterns kept coming back.

01Following keywords instead of logicTreats every question that sounds related as relevant, instead of reasoning about which one answers the ask02Missing the unwritten rulesTrusts the more detailed source over the one a professional would trust

Note · Examples and numbers below are invented to show each pattern. They are not actual problems.

AI picks the internal summary because it has more detail, but when the two sources do not tie, the compiled financial statements win

○What a professional does: trusts this one when the two do not tie
→Nobody writes this rule down: when the numbers do not tie and you cannot see how the internal ones were built, the compiled statements win, even unaudited
