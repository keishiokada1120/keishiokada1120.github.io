Hotel SaaS GTM example · How I built it

Real market data

I used AI to turn raw market data into a live answer on where a hotel SaaS company should sell first

The ask. A fictional SaaS company sells U.S. hotels an AI that answers guest messages. Which hotel segment should it sell to first?

How I built the analysis

1Raw data

Euromonitor hotel sales by segment from 2012 to 2031, company and brand shares, and a Mintel survey of 2,000 U.S. adults

2Built with Claude

Read the raw exports, tied every segment to one total, matched the survey to the segments and scored each segment on five criteria

3Live answer

Change a weight in the scoring below, and the ranking, the chart and the headline update on their own

My part: the question, what counts and how much, and the final check where models tend to slip
→The answer: start with upscale, a $56B segment expected to grow 4.4% a year to 2031, where five groups hold 93% of sales, then mid market

Source · Euromonitor Passport and Mintel, with details below · The company is fictional
